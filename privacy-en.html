JotStamp Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026
Contact: Trần Hoàng Nam — hoangnamttq@gmail.com

What the app does

JotStamp records work photos and short videos, adds visible stamps, organizes local jobs and produces local photo reports. It does not require a JotStamp account.

Media and job data

Photos, videos, logos, notes, job details, capture dates and any chosen location details are stored in the app's protected storage on your device. Rendering, video stamping and PDF generation run on the device. This version has no developer-operated media server, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, AI service or automatic upload of your work to the developer. We do not sell media or use it to train models.

Permissions and location

Camera access is used for capture. Microphone access is requested only for your choice to record with audio. Import uses Apple's system picker for selected files, without requesting access to your full photo library. Adding an exported copy to Photos uses add-only permission. Location is optional and used while the app is active. We retain its available accuracy and capture context alongside the media. No background-location mode is requested.

Automatic address lookup

If you enable this optional setting, coordinates are sent to Apple's geocoding service to obtain an address. This service may use a network connection and is governed by Apple's terms and privacy practices. Location lookup may be approximate or unavailable; capture is still usable. You can turn the setting off.

Purchases

Apple processes payment and manages your App Store account. The app reads verified purchase and subscription information to determine Pro access. We do not receive your card number from the app. Apple's purchase, refund and account services have their own privacy practices.

Backups and sharing

Persistent originals, saved videos, job records and branding can be included in Apple system backups, according to your device settings. Temporary export files are not intended for system backup. You can create your own backup package through Files. When you choose Photos, iCloud Drive, email or another share destination, those services may store or transfer a copy according to your choices and their policies. Media does not necessarily remain only on this device after those actions.

Exporting without location

The new-export option removes structured GPS/address fields from both the visible stamp and export metadata. It does not automatically redact location already present in source pixels, report titles, captions or free-text notes. Inspect your output before sending it. Sharing an original or an already generated video shares that file's existing contents.

Support and diagnostics

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you deliberately send. No photo, coordinate, receipt or log is attached automatically. We use support correspondence to answer your request and investigate issues, retaining it only as needed for support, dispute resolution or legal obligations. You may request deletion through the support address. Do not send passwords or payment credentials. Apple may provide diagnostic information or TestFlight feedback under its settings and your choices; this app does not operate an additional telemetry service.

Website

The public support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security, including visits without a GitHub account. Technical request information may also include browser details and access time. Our pages contain no advertising pixels, remote fonts, analytics scripts or upload forms. Provider retention follows GitHub's operational, security and legal policies; we do not promise a fixed deletion period for its logs. See https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement for details. Opening the website does not upload your JotStamp photos or jobs.

Control and retention

You can edit, export or delete work in the app and change permissions in iOS Settings. Deleting work in JotStamp does not delete copies in Photos, Files, backups or recipients' systems. Deleting all app data does not cancel an Apple subscription. Uninstalling the app or losing the device can remove local data without a usable backup. Restore Purchases restores paid access, not photos or jobs. We cannot recover a file that was never saved or sent to us.

Security and changes

The app uses iOS application storage protections and avoids overwriting originals during editing. No storage system can eliminate every risk; maintain backups for important work. Material policy changes will be reflected in an updated effective date. Contact us about privacy questions, access to support correspondence or deletion requests. Applicable legal rights remain unaffected.
